1. What are Grok Bot templates?
Grok Bot is one of the best agentic platforms out there, powered by the latest Grok models
So, a template is a blueprint of a bot that you can share with others through a link
A template includes:
Bot instructions (identity, description)
Skills - processes the bot can repeat
Routines schedule (automated routine tasks on a set schedule)
Selected memories (specific chosen memory)
First-party plugins and third-party service integrations like Gmail, Slack, GitHub, etc.
Basically you get the blueprint, and on top of that version you build whatever you need
And it actually improves your user experience by making the final output result significantly better
After running some analysis on existing templates, I realized the real value usually comes down to:
1. Persistence
Repetitive ongoing work that can be automated and set to run on a schedule automatically
2. Connected tools
Plugins and third-party service integrations plugged in to make the bot truly automated
3. Repeated context
Accumulated context that helps the bot work in a truly automated way, using the right context from step one to run in the background
4. Background execution
Working in the background with zero human involvement, running a continuous loop of steps toward a specific output result
5. Accumulated memory
Built-up memory of edits, assigned tasks, completed tasks, improvements, and everything the bot has absorbed - all of it feeding back into better efficiency and results over time
So really, a useful template is one where switching back to a regular Grok chat would make your workflow noticeably worse
How to create a template?
Open your bot in Grok Bot
Hit "Share" in the top right corner, or type in the chat "Create a template of yourself that I can share with somebody else"
Grok Bot will automatically prepare an unpublished draft from your bot's configuration (it tries to include what others need and exclude your personal data)
Review what's included: instructions, memories, skills, routines, plugins
Before publishing - remove API keys, internal URLs, client data, anything confidential
Hit Publish → you get a link
Share it on X, and then earn a reward (add a paid partnership tag to every tweet with a link to your bot)
Overall, creating one isn't hard, but it's important to understand that you need to build a bot that's actually good, one that without it, a person would have to rebuild everything from scratch
If your bot is just a single prompt in Grok Bot, it doesn't really solve anything, and barely anyone is going to use it
Also, if you're building a complex bot, it's important to include setup instructions, because some dependencies won't transfer with the template - things like MCP servers, custom scripts, etc.
So, when you're creating it and you hit Share, review the draft and check it thoroughly, and also remove any confidential information
How to promote your own template?
Distribution is one of the most important parts, even if your bot is actually useful
So, I'll share a few tips and formats you can use:
Find the core pain point your template solves, and use that in your content
Show the final output - as a screen recording or result and share it on X
Write detailed articles where you use your template as part of a workflow
Build setup instructions into the bot itself, and share that in your content
Write a detailed review article on the bot - setup after installation, connecting plugins step by step, and a demo of it actually working
Obviously, the best distribution channel here is X. It's mandatory for the rewards program
From the official Rewards program requirements, there are also a few important points:
Your post on X with the template link must stay public and unedited for at least 30 days after the period you received your reward for
You must add the X paid partnership label to every post promoting an eligible template
The template is shared through a link in the format x.ai/bot/ + a 21-character token
You can also use the Grok Bot marketplace for additional promotion that's separate from the Rewards Program
